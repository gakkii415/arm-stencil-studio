# Right-arm model source

- Author: Dan Ulrich
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal (CC0-1.0)
- Official bundle: https://download.blender.org/demo/asset-bundles/human-base-meshes/human-base-meshes-bundle-v1.4.1.zip
- Official license information: https://www.blender.org/download/demo-files/
- Original asset: `GEO-body_male_realistic`, evaluated at Multiresolution level 2.
- Selected original sculpt face sets: 20 (upper arm), 11 (forearm), 10 and 84–103 (hand/fingers).
- The artist scan surface is retained. Processing consists of uniform scaling, rigid rotation, a cap over the original shoulder opening, and triangle subdivision at paint/UV boundaries.
- Shoulder-to-wrist reference landmark distance is set to 540 mm. Hand/fingers and the shoulder cap are display context only.
- Circumferences are measured from horizontal mesh intersections every 5 mm and used for an approximate wrap stencil. This is not an individual body measurement or an exact developable surface.
